Our latest paper is out! "Comparison of calorimetry and NMR relaxometry measurements of epoxy cure kinetics" is now published in the *Journal of Polymer Science*.

<!--more-->

Congratulations to Olivia, Kayla, Joyce, and Jack! The paper compares differential scanning calorimetry and single-sided NMR relaxometry (*T*2) as complementary, non-destructive techniques for tracking epoxy cure kinetics.

Read the full paper: [doi.org/10.1002/pola.70292](https://doi.org/10.1002/pola.70292)
