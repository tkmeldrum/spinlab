Congratulations to senior Kylie McCoy, who attended the [ACS Spring 2026](https://www.acs.org/events/spring.html) national meeting in Atlanta, GA, where she presented her research and won an **Outstanding Student Presenter Award** for exemplary research!

<!--more-->

Kylie received [Charles Center](https://www.wm.edu/as/charlescenter/) Research in Motion funding to attend and present her talk, *"Mitigating overestimation in rehydroxylation dating: residual hydroxyls and the role of firing temperature in ancient pottery."* We are so proud of her and thrilled to share that she will be continuing at W&M as a Master's student in the Spinlab. Congratulations, Kylie!
