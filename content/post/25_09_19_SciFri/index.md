The Spinlab participated in William & Mary's third annual Sci-Fri: an open night of science for the community!<!--more--> Group members presented posters (Shalom and Noah), showcased molecular dynamics simulations (Thomas), helped NMR apprentices identify the color of Starburst *without opening the package,* and volunteered broadly at the event. Plus, Tyler has been the Sci-Fri organizer three years running! Thanks for sharing science with the broader Williamsburg community.
