Congratulations to group alumni Shinjer and Sam: their paper, [*RAMM: A Robotic, Autonomous Magnetic field Mapper*](https://dx.doi.org/10.1016/j.ohx.2025.e00700) is now published at the open-source journal HardwareX. This paper talks about a robot we designed and constructed to measure and map magnetic fields produces by our single-sided (and other) magnets. Congratulations!

At the time of writing, Sam is a second-year PhD student in Bioengineering at UC Berkeley and Shinjer is in his first year of medical school at Eastern Virginia Medical School.
