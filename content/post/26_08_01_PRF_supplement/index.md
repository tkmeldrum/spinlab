We're happy to share that spinlab has received a supplemental award from the American Chemical Society's Petroleum Research Fund (ACS PRF), building on our existing PRF Undergraduate Research Grant (69767-UR6).

<!--more-->

The supplement will support instrumentation for the group and help sustain research capacity more broadly within the Department. The award targets the development of sustainable support for higher education and will increase capacity in experimental thermodynamics at W&M.

You can read more about this round of ACS PRF supplemental funding [here](https://cen.acs.org/acs-news/petroleum-research-fund-continues-supplemental/104/web/2026/07).
