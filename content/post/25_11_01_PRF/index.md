title: PRF Grant Funded
date: 2025-11-01

We are excited to announce that our proposal to the [ACS Petroleum Research Fund](https://www.acs.org/funding/grants/petroleum-research-fund.html) has been funded! The project, "Machine learning in NMR relaxometry for characterization of complex mixtures," will begin June 1, 2026.

<!--more-->

This grant will support several undergraduate students as they develop and apply machine learning tools to NMR relaxometry data. We look forward to sharing more as the project gets underway!
