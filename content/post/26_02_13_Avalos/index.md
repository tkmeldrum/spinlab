We were delighted to host [Prof. Claudia Avalos](https://as.nyu.edu/faculty/claudia-avalos.html) of NYU for a visit to William & Mary!

<!--more-->

Prof. Avalos gave a seminar, "Lighting up Magnetic Resonance: The Role of Spin in Optical Pathways," and met with W&M faculty. She also joined the Spinlab for group meeting, where our seniors gave overviews of the exciting projects underway in the lab. It was a wonderful visit!
